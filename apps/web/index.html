Requirements & availability

Everything works in every modern browser. On-device inference needs Chrome 139+ with Gemini Nano enabled.

Cloud lane

Works in any browser, right now. Firebase AI Logic sends prompts to gemini-3.5-flash-lite via App Check. No setup, no extensions, no flags.

On-device lane

Needs Chrome 139+ on a desktop with Gemini Nano enabled (chrome://flags/#optimization-guide-on-device-model → Enabled). Without it, the cloud lane runs alone and every screen honestly says why the on-device lane is idle.

Don't have Gemini Nano? That is fine. A labelled reference run from the author's laptop fills every chart and policy rule. The app never pretends — it shows exactly what is available on your device and what is not.

Held-out results on one laptop

The policy was compiled from calibration tasks only, then replayed on 93 held-out tasks measured on both models. Intel Iris Xe, 16 GB, Chrome 158.

On this laptop's integrated GPU, Gemini Nano is slower than the cloud for every task type. At the interactive 8-second budget, the policy keeps nothing on-device. That is the honest finding — and the reason Tandem exists.

Without measurement, a developer ships on-device inference that times out in production. With a 30-second budget for background work, 86% stays on-device at 100% quality. The budget is the developer's choice; Tandem shows what each choice costs.

What was built

Five TypeScript packages, two web apps, one measurement pipeline — built in nine days on the Firebase Spark plan with no server-side code.

5 packages

tandem-guard, tandem-ai, tandem-bench, tandem-policy, tandem-router — each independently tested, together forming a measured routing stack.

192 calibration runs

8 task types × 12 synthetic tasks × 2 lanes, with deterministic validators scoring every answer. 93 held-out tasks replayed for validation.

263-case adversarial corpus

Tandem Guard tested against Aadhaar, PAN, GSTIN, IFSC, UPI, phone and bank numbers disguised with Indic digits, spacing, Cyrillic lookalikes and base64.

Wilson 95% intervals

Every routing rule carries a Wilson lower bound on pass rate — not a point estimate, not a guess. Thresholds were fixed before any data existed.

SHA-256 hash chain

Every routing decision produces a receipt. Receipts are chained: tamper with one and every receipt after it breaks. Verify in-browser.

Real-time policy swap

The compiled policy ships through Firebase Remote Config with onConfigUpdate. Open apps get the new policy without a reload.

Questions from judges and developers

Do I need Gemini Nano to try it?
No. Every screen works without it, the on-device lane says why it is idle, and a labelled reference run from the author's laptop fills the charts.
Is anything simulated?
No. Every badge shows the inferenceSource Firebase AI Logic reports, and every figure on this page comes from a result file in the repository.
What leaves my device?
Nothing for on-device answers. For cloud answers, the receipt shows the exact payload and the bytes sent. Receipts hold metadata only, never content.
How does a rule become local-first?
Four checks on the probe data: at least 8 tasks, a 95% lower bound of 75% on-device, within 5 points of the cloud, and a p95 answer time inside your latency budget.
Can I use the router in my own app?
Yes. The router is a TypeScript package over Firebase AI Logic: create it with your Firebase app and policy, call run with a task and input, and read the receipt.
Which Firebase products does it use?
AI Logic hybrid inference, App Check with reCAPTCHA v3, anonymous Authentication, Firestore and Remote Config, which ships the policy to open apps in real time.
